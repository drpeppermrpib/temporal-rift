## Why this order

1. **10.1 water body** — geometry first: shore collision + spawn guard must exist before anything floats; the blob dodges camp, N/W corridors, and spawn walks.
2. **10.2 dock** — the boat needs a moor target and the shore needs a landmark before mount code.
3. **10.3 skiff** — wreck→repair→mount mirrors the proven bike flow; wave gate lands with the boat, not after.
4. **10.4 isle** — the reward needs the boat to be reachable; content after traversal.
5. **10.5 water rules** — harassment/leash polish and stall-proof smokes once boats and threats can actually meet.
6. **10.6 balance** — drift/drain/cost tune with everything live.
7. **10.7 gate** — 4.4.0 BETA once all five bases still bank plus the lake loop holds.
