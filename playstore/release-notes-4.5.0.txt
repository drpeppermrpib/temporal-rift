v4.5.0 – Controller update
• Gamepad support (Bluetooth/USB): left stick moves, right stick aims, triggers fire and charge the Nova Beam. Menus, shop, skill tree and settings work with the D-pad.
• Touch controls auto-hide while a controller is in use and come back when you touch the screen. New setting: Auto / Always show / Always hide.
• Refreshed hero: new armor, ash cloak and scarf, detailed rifle, smoother walk, recoil, hurt and ascension animations.
